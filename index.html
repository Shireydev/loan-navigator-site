Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

DS Apps & Products respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Loan Navigator mobile application.

Information You Enter

Loan Navigator allows you to enter financial information such as loan balances, interest rates, payment amounts, property estimates, and other scenario details. This information is used to perform calculations and provide app functionality.

How Information Is Used

Information entered into the app may be used to:

  • Calculate estimated payments and payoff schedules.
  • Compare loan and refinancing scenarios.
  • Create repayment plans and debt-tracking summaries.
  • Save scenarios or preferences on your device.
  • Improve app stability, performance, and usability.

Financial Information

Loan Navigator is an educational and planning tool. The app does not provide financial, legal, tax, lending, or investment advice. Calculations are estimates and may differ from actual lender terms, taxes, insurance, fees, or other costs.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Loan Navigator may use third-party advertising, analytics, or technical services. These providers may process limited device, diagnostic, advertising, or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Storage and Security

Certain app information may be stored locally on your device. Reasonable efforts are made to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

Loan Navigator is not directed toward children under 13, and DS Apps & Products does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You may remove locally stored app information by deleting saved scenarios, clearing application data, or uninstalling the app, depending on your device and app version.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised as the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to DS Apps & Products using the contact information in the Support section below.
