Loan Navigator Privacy Policy

Loan Navigator respects your privacy. This page explains how information is handled when you use the Loan Navigator mobile application.

Information We Collect

Loan Navigator may collect information you voluntarily enter into the app, such as loan amounts, interest rates, payment information, and debt details.

How Information Is Used

Information entered into the app is used to provide calculations, estimates, repayment plans, and related app functionality.

Advertising

Loan Navigator may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services. Those services may collect device or usage information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Loan Navigator is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact DS Apps & Products.
